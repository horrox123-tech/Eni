The Vision of Our Future

Enioluwa Orolugbagbe, commonly referred to as "Mr. Oroks" (or "Eni" by his friends), is a passionate politician and community leader who especially believes power is held by the people.

As a politician, I am interested in building a new society where people have equal opportunities and face no discrimination based on their background or identity. I believe we must work together improve the community, "I live to serve". My work is not limited to a province or country, how do I make a difference by helping only myself and surroundings?

Core Pillars

Enioluwa Orolugbagbe's vision for the future is built upon three core pillars:

  • Feminism/ Gender Equality
  • Society's Progress and Prosperity
  • Global Safety and Humanitarian aid

    • "The government lives to serve its people, and not vice versa."
