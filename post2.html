Meet Mr. Oroks

Mr. Oroks' journey in politics and community service was driven by the deep desire to make a change and serve his community. Eni was raised in Nigeria, until about late 2024, as a child in Nigeria he was present during a brutal time in Nigeria history, the #EndSARS movement; a time were protesters were killed during protests for protesting against police brutality and corruption. A few years later, he also saw people performing an act known as "Jungle Justice" a process that usually involves crowd punishment by assaulting people, humiliation by forcing them to dance while beating them, or sometimes putting them in a tire to burn, this revealed to him that society can't change if the government doesn't want them to, this established one of his core pillars(Society's Progress and Prosperity). After he came to a little city in Saskatchewan, Canada called Regina, he saw how much better, but not perfect, considering how much better it is that Nigeria another country it helped him realize another one of his core pillars(Global Safety and Humanitarian aid). He realized Feminism/ Gender Equality as a core pillar after reading a book called "I am a Feminist" by Monique Polak, this book helped him understand the importance of feminism and how we should all strive to become a feminist society. During his years in Regina he's written an essay about some problems faced with society here in Canada such as Illiteracy and Homelessness rate in the community, and right now he plans on making a book report about Monique Polak's book, "I am a Feminist". As a child he was a member of air cadets the 41 Hercules Squadron, as well as a regular volunteer at the Regina Humane Society, he was 2nd in Saskatchewan for Grade 8's for the CJMC in 2025, as well as participated in the Optimist Contest.

My Values

I believe in feminism, equality, opportunity, change, service, and justice. I believe that everyone deserves to be treated with dignity, and that the government should listen and serve the people.
